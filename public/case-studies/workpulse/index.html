THE DETAILS MATTER

Useful answers.
No guesswork.

Who is WorkPulse designed for?

Indian small and medium businesses managing people across shops, branches and field locations. The supplied examples show site-based construction and electrical work.

Do workers and supervisors see the same experience?

They share the operational foundation, but their screens and actions reflect their responsibilities and permissions. A worker can review personal attendance and assigned work; a supervisor can coordinate the sites within their access scope.

How does a task become complete?

The assigned worker records work and submits it for review. An authorised reviewer approves it or requests rework. Approval moves it to Completed and closes further activity.

What connects attendance and payroll?

The backend includes attendance-based earnings and payroll review. The web console brings worker readiness, advances and payments into the monthly workflow before generation and locking.

Are these actual product screenshots?

Yes. All 23 application images were supplied for this case study. They show the captured product state and are not evidence of customer growth or measured business impact.
